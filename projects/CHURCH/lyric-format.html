How the lyrics are read
  • Blocks separated by a blank line are sections. Verse numbers such as 1. or 2) are removed.
  • A section labeled Refren, Cor or Chorus is the chorus. If there are numbered verses and an unlabeled section, that section is the chorus.
  • With no numbers or labels, a section that appears more than once is the chorus.
  • The chorus is placed after every verse. If it already follows every verse, nothing is added.
  • Sections are separated by a line containing ---.
